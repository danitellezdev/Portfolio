Cookie Policy

Current status

This portfolio has no active analytics or advertising tools. The theme preference is not saved in a cookie.

01 / Concepts

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website may store in a browser to remember information or provide a feature. Similar storage or access technologies are also covered here when they serve an equivalent purpose.

02 / Categories

Technologies currently used

Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and other analytics or advertising services are not currently enabled. The portfolio code does not save the theme preference or other browsing data in first-party cookies. A code review does not replace checking cookies the browser may receive when visiting the deployed site or submitting a form to an external provider.

  • Optional analytics: not active.
  • Advertising or tracking: not used.
  • Third-party technical cookies: this policy does not claim that Formspree sets them on this site without browser verification; see “Technical cookies and the form”.

Cookie names, purposes, and durations will be listed once confirmed on the deployed site and in current provider documentation.

03 / Operation

Technical cookies and the form

The contact form sends data to Formspree when “Send message” is selected. Formspree may receive technical data associated with the request to provide and protect its service; its Privacy Policy describes its general practices. That policy alone does not establish that Formspree places a cookie in the browser when this portfolio is visited. Any cookie name, duration, and necessity will be published only after checking the deployed site during both browsing and form interaction.

Choosing a light or dark theme on these pages changes the appearance for the current visit and is not saved in a cookie.

04 / Future services

Future Analytics Services

Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, and similar tools are not currently active. Before any optional analytics service is enabled, this policy will be updated with its provider, purpose, technologies, duration, recipients, and any international transfers.

Where consent is required, optional scripts and cookies will remain blocked until the visitor makes a choice. Clear options to accept, reject, or configure categories will be provided, with rejection as accessible as acceptance and a persistent way to withdraw or change the choice. The policy and cookie inventory will be updated before activation.

05 / Preferences

Managing cookies

You can review, block, or delete cookies in your browser settings. Because analytics and advertising are not active, this site does not currently display a consent panel for those categories. If an optional technology requiring consent is added, an accessible preference control, including a way to withdraw consent, will be provided before it is activated.

The presence and settings of third-party technical cookies must be checked on the deployed site. This information will also be reviewed if the form or hosting provider changes.
