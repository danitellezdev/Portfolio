01 / Controller

Who handles your data

The controller of personal data submitted through this portfolio is Daniel Téllez. The stated location is Andalucía, Spain.

danitellez.dev@gmail.com Andalucía, Spain

02 / Purposes

Why data is used

Data is used to receive, manage, and reply to professional inquiries submitted through the form, and to prevent abusive submissions. It is not used for newsletters, advertising, or marketing messages.

For inquiries about a possible service, the legal basis is taking pre-contractual steps at your request (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). For other inquiries, the basis is the legitimate interest in managing and replying to direct communications (Article 6(1)(f)). Preventing abusive submissions is also based on the legitimate interest in protecting the form.

No automated decisions or profiling with legal or similarly significant effects are made about people who contact the site.

03 / Contact form

What information is received

Name, email address, and message are required to receive and respond to an inquiry. If you do not provide them, the form cannot process the request. Technical information associated with a submission may also be generated, such as its date, IP address, browser, or spam-prevention and security data, depending on how the form provider operates and its privacy policy.

Please provide only information needed for your inquiry. Do not include unnecessary sensitive data or information about other people.

04 / Form provider

Formspree

Messages submitted through the contact form are processed through Formspree, the contact form provider. Formspree receives the fields and provides message receipt and delivery. It may also process technical data, such as an IP address or browser, to operate and protect its service. Read the Formspree Privacy Policy.

Whether Formspree formally acts as a processor and which contractual terms apply depend on the terms and agreement in force for the account. No agreement or contractual safeguard is assumed here unless it has been verified.

05 / Hosting

GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs and stores the IP addresses of visitors to GitHub Pages sites for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and information about GitHub Pages. GitHub manages its internal security logs; the portfolio owner does not control them.

06 / Data location

International transfers

Depending on Formspree's infrastructure, data may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Formspree's privacy policy describes its general locations and practices. No specific international transfer mechanism is stated here because the mechanism applicable to this account has not been verified. Formspree's current terms and safeguards should be checked before using the form with real data.

Daniel Téllez does not sell personal data or use it for his own marketing campaigns. This information will be updated if the form or hosting provider changes.

07 / Retention

How long data is kept

Copies of messages under Daniel Téllez's control are kept while the inquiry is being handled and for no more than 12 months after the last substantive interaction. They are then deleted. If a legal obligation or the handling or defence of claims requires it, the data will be restricted for the applicable legal period and deleted when that period ends.

Formspree may retain submissions and technical data under the account configuration and service terms. Its public policy does not set one fixed period for this account; its account settings and applicable agreement should be checked and deletion configured. The 12-month period above applies only to copies controlled by the data controller and does not describe or replace the provider's own retention.

08 / Your rights

Access, correction, and control

You may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, erasure, restriction of processing, objection, and, where applicable, data portability. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; this does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, email danitellez.dev@gmail.com and describe your request. A response will generally be provided within one month. For complex or numerous requests, this period may be extended by up to two further months; you will be informed of the extension and reasons within the first month. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

09 / Protection

How data is protected

Access to messages is limited and the form and technical services are used only for the purposes described. GitHub and Formspree apply their own security measures under their policies and terms. No internet-connected channel can guarantee zero risk, so please do not send unnecessary confidential information.
